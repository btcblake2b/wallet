In a hurry? Five answers

Do I need to buy anything to take this course?

No. The course is free and open source, and the app has no accounts, no paid tiers and no token. You only need funds if you want to practise sending.

Is this a Bitcoin wallet?

It is a wallet for bitcoin-blake2b, a separate experimental network that shares Bitcoin's design but has its own rules and its own coins. Read module 1 → the network before handling real funds.

Can the author of the app recover my funds?

No, by design. The keys are generated and encrypted on your device. Your recovery phrase is the only backup — see module 3.

What does the app send to the internet?

Only the queries needed to read the chain (and to broadcast a transaction), to one public API, optionally with community mirrors. Module 4 lists them one by one and shows how to turn the mirrors off.

Is the course available in other languages?

The course is written in English only, on purpose: it is the canonical text and stays current. Use your browser's built-in translation (Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari all offer it) for any language.
